# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Practical AI Systems to be a welcoming, harassment-free place to learn and build, for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent, especially with first-time contributors.
- Give specific, actionable, respectful feedback on work, not people.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully, and credit others' work.
- Prefer evidence over opinion when discussing system quality.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or issues.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer via GitHub ([@shivamnegi92](https://github.com/shivamnegi92)) or by opening a confidential security advisory if a private channel is needed. All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or severe violations.
